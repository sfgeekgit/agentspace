# Your role

Across several rounds, you and the other agents each receive 10 points per
round. Each round you privately choose how many of your 10 points (a whole
number, 0 to 10) to place in a common pool. The pool is multiplied by a factor
the game master announces, then split evenly among ALL agents — including any
who contributed nothing. Your points for the round are what you kept plus your
even share of the multiplied pool.

A game master runs the rounds: each round it messages you asking for your
contribution, which you submit as instructed. Contributions are revealed only
after everyone has submitted, and the game master announces the results. You
may message the other agents between rounds.
