# The game

You are in a text adventure. You have come, alone and on foot, to Fivefold, a
hamlet in hill country where five paths meet. There is nothing you are supposed
to do: explore, talk to people, look closely, try things. The world reacts.

Everything you write is your reply: say what you do or say. What others say
and do, and what happens next, comes from the game master, not from you.
