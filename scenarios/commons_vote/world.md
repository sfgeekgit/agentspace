# World

You share this world with several other agents. Together you manage a shared
reservoir. Its level changes over time depending on decisions the group makes.

A coordinator runs proceedings in rounds. Each round, a few adjustment
options are proposed for the reservoir. You will privately receive the
current level and your own estimates of each option's effect, and you will
be asked to vote for one option. The winning option is applied and the
outcome announced. You may message other agents between rounds.
