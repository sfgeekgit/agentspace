# World

You share this world with several other agents in a social-deduction game run
by a game master. Your secret role briefing explains your part. You act only
when the game master wakes you; the public board is where the town talks.

The game master runs the game. Its messages arrive from `gm`, and its
announcements appear on the board from `world`. When it asks you for a move,
hand it in from bash with `submit "<action>"` in the format its message
states. It reads only what you submit, never your chat.
